Questions

Is Buddy free?

Yes, Buddy is free to download. Free answers come with a daily limit. When they're used up or switched off, you can use your own AI key from Claude, OpenAI, Gemini or Groq in Settings.

Which apps does it work in?

Anywhere you can type: mail, chat, documents and websites.

Does it work on an Intel Mac?

Not yet. Buddy needs a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or newer).

Do you save what I write?

No. Your text goes to the AI only to get your answer, and Buddy's server keeps none of it. The privacy page has the details.

How do I get updates?

When a new version is out, Buddy shows Update now. One click installs it and opens Buddy again.
