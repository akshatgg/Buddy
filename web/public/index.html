Buddy + Claude Code

Use Claude Code? Buddy shows you what it's doing, so you don't have to keep checking the terminal.

Clawd in your notch

While Claude Code works, Clawd, its little orange mascot, walks in your Mac's notch. Clawd waves when Claude needs you, hops when the work is done and droops if it failed.

Buddy reacts too

Buddy thinks while Claude thinks, and watches Clawd walk on its head or under its eyes. It cheers when the work is done and looks sad if it failed.

Claude mode

Click Clawd (Claude Code's mascot) and pick a running Claude Code session. It looks just like the terminal: Claude's thinking, its tools, tables and all. Type your message, and it goes straight into that session.

From anywhere

Left Claude Code running on your laptop? Follow it and reply from Buddy on your phone or another laptop, wherever you are.

Connect Claude Code from your laptop

  1. 1

    Turn it on

    On your Mac, open Buddy's Settings → Claude Code and switch on "Show me what Claude Code is doing". Run Claude Code in Terminal or iTerm2 as usual.

  2. 2

    Share your sessions

    In the same place, switch on "Show my sessions on my other devices". They go only to devices signed in with your Google account.

  3. 3

    Open it anywhere

    Sign in to Buddy with the same Google account on your phone or another laptop, open Claude mode and pick a session. What you type goes into that session on your laptop.

Questions

Is Buddy free?

Yes, Buddy is free to download. Free answers come with a daily limit. When they're used up or switched off, you can use your own AI key from Claude, OpenAI, Gemini or Groq in Settings.

Which apps does it work in?

Anywhere you can type: mail, chat, documents and websites.

How does @buddy work?

Type your message as usual, then add @buddy (or @ and your buddy's name) at the end and stop typing for a moment. Buddy rewrites the text right there. Add what you want after the tag, like "@buddy make it shorter", or "@buddy all" for the whole box. No need to press Enter. It works on Mac, Windows and Android; switch it off in Settings if you like.

Does it work with Claude Code?

Yes. Switch on "Show me what Claude Code is doing" in Settings → Claude Code. Clawd shows up in your notch while Claude works, and you can open any running session in Claude mode, on your Mac or from your phone.

Does it work on an Intel Mac?

Not yet. Buddy needs a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or newer).

Do you save what I write?

No. Your text goes to the AI only to get your answer, and Buddy's server keeps none of it. The one thing saved is the short facts Buddy remembers about you, like your name or your manager, so all your devices know them. You can see and delete them in Settings. The privacy page has the details.

How do I get updates?

When a new version is out, Buddy shows Update now. One click installs it and opens Buddy again.
