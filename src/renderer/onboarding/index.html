Hi! Pick your buddy

You can change this any time in Settings.

Let your buddy type for you

To read the text you select and paste answers into Gmail, WhatsApp and other apps, your Mac needs to allow Accessibility for Buddy.

Click the button, then switch Buddy on in the list that opens.

Let your buddy see your screen

For Check screen, your buddy takes a screenshot of the window you are in. Allow Screen Recording for Buddy. You can skip this and allow it later.

Connect an AI

Your buddy uses an AI to write. Pick one and paste your API key. You can also do this later in Settings.
