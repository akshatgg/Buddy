Privacy

Buddy helps you write. It doesn't keep what you write. Here is everything it keeps, everything it doesn't, and where your text goes.

Updated 9 October 2026

What Buddy keeps

When you sign in with Google, Buddy's server keeps one small record about you:

  • your name and email address from Google;
  • when you first signed in, and when you last asked Buddy something;
  • how many free answers you used today;
  • whether the account is blocked.

This record lives in Cloud Firestore, a database on Google Cloud. Only Buddy's server can read it. It is used to count free answers and to stop misuse, and for nothing else. It is never sold or shared.

When you're signed in, the server also keeps what Buddy remembers about you: at most 50 short facts, like "Your boss is Mr. Sharma.", so that your Mac, Windows PC, Android phone and iPhone all know the same things. Buddy never keeps a password, PIN, OTP or card, bank or ID number as a fact. You can see every fact, delete one, or delete them all in Settings → Memory, and turn off "Learn about me from chats". They are kept in the same database, and only Buddy's server can read them.

If you switch on "Show my sessions on my other devices" in Settings → Claude Code, your Mac sends the list of its running Claude Code sessions to Buddy's server, so that you can open them from your phone or another laptop signed in with the same Google account. While you have a session open there, its latest view (what Claude wrote and did) passes through the server, replacing the one before, and what you type goes back to your Mac. It isn't kept as a history, and switching it off stops it.

What Buddy never keeps

  • What you write, and what you ask Buddy to do (apart from the facts it remembers about you, above).
  • Your screenshots from Check screen.
  • The answers Buddy gives you.
  • Your AI key, if you add one. It stays on your computer, encrypted, and is sent only to that AI.

Where your text goes

To write an answer, Buddy sends your text (and, for Check screen, a picture of the window you're in) to an AI: the one Buddy's free answers use, or the one whose key you added (Anthropic's Claude, OpenAI, Google Gemini or Groq). It is sent only to get that answer. That company's own privacy policy covers what it does with requests.

What Buddy can do on your Mac

  • Accessibility: to copy the text you selected and paste the answer back where you were typing. Buddy never reads password fields.
  • Screen Recording: only when you press Check screen, to take one picture of the window you're in.

You can switch either off any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Deleting your data

Forgetting everything in Settings → Memory deletes what Buddy remembers about you, on all your devices. Email akshatg9636@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, and your record and what Buddy remembers about you are deleted. Signing out of Buddy removes your sign-in from your computer.
